# Story, hook & tension

## 36-second shape (one episode)
| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook**: odd specific detail, forbidden rule, impossible fact, or ticking clock |
| 3–12 s | Setup: who, where, what they want (one image per sentence) |
| 12–26 s | Escalation: two beats, each raises stakes or deepens the oddness |
| 26–32 s | Turn: a reveal that answers the small question but opens a bigger one |
| 32–36 s | Cliffhanger line + comment-bait question |

## Hook formulas (pick one per episode)
1. Impossible knowledge ("उसने नाम बताया ही नहीं था")  2. Forbidden rule ("मत खोलना")  3. Mismatch ("घंटों बाद भी गर्म")  4. Ticking clock ("आख़िरी बस")  5. Wrong place/time ("वहाँ कोई गाँव था ही नहीं")

## Rules
- One mystery per episode. Each episode must **reveal one new fact and open one new question**. Keep a plant→pay-off ledger in `story_bible.md`.
- Narration: sentences ≤ 9 words, concrete senses (गर्म, ठंडी हवा, चरमराहट), no abstract words, no explaining the twist.
- Measured pace with Kokoro `hm_omega` at speed 0.92: **~2.4 words/second** (85 words ≈ 36 s). Plan word count = seconds × 2.3.
- Tension tools: short sentence after long one; repeated phrase; **0.6–0.9 s silence before a reveal**; drop music at the reveal; end on the question, not the answer.
- Cliffhanger types: new fact that contradicts the setup · a choice · a sound/phone call · a place that should not exist.
- Series: 5-episode arcs; next episode opens with a 2-second recap. Resolve every arc with an emotional pay-off, not just a trick.
- Family-safe suspense only (no gore/violence/self-harm; no real brands, people or crimes).

## Output format
A table: `line | start–end s | on-screen action | SFX cue | caption text`, plus word count and a plant/pay-off note. Hand to voice-direction and shot-camera-motion.
