# Story bible — Series 1: "आख़िरी बस" (family-safe suspense, Hindi)

**Premise:** Rohan takes the last night bus home. An old woman who should be a stranger knows his name and gives him a tiffin: "घर पहुँचने से पहले इसे मत खोलना."
**Tone:** quiet, eerie, warm underneath. No gore, no real crime, no real brands/people/places.
**Cast:** रोहन (student) · अम्मा (mysterious old woman) · ड्राइवर (knows more than he says).
**Format:** 5 episodes, 35–45 s each, each ends on a question. Episode N+1 opens with a 2-second recap.

## Arc (`[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]` — human may change)
1. आख़िरी बस — amma knows his name, gives tiffin, bus stops where there is no village.
2. गर्म टिफ़िन — tiffin is still warm; phone rings: "तू कहाँ है? बस तो कल रात निकल चुकी है."
3. ड्राइवर का राज़ — driver has run this route for 20 years; every night one passenger boards.
4. टिफ़िन खुला — Rohan breaks the rule: an old photo and a key.
5. घर — emotional pay-off: the key opens a family home Rohan never knew; amma's last delivery was a letter that never reached him. (Resolve warmly.)
